Privacy

The history stores the domain, the check results and their evidence. It stores nothing about the visitor.

An entry is kept for 90 days after it was last seen; the latest entry keeps its predecessor; a domain nobody checks for 90 days is deleted with all its entries.

Rate limiters and the throttle are held in memory only, keyed by address or domain; nothing of them is written to disk.
